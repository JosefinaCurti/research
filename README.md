# Nuclear Projects on Tribal Lands: Federal Incentives Overview
 
> **Prepared for:** Pre-call briefing — Partner discussion on Tribal Land nuclear deployment  
> **Date:** June 2026  
> **Status:** Current as of OBBB Act (signed July 4, 2025) and IRS guidance through 2026
 
---
 
## Executive Summary
 
Nuclear energy projects sited on Tribal Lands sit at the intersection of two powerful federal policy tracks: (1) robust nuclear-specific tax credits that largely survived the 2025 One Big Beautiful Bill Act (OBBB), and (2) Tribal-specific mechanisms — most notably **Direct Pay (Elective Pay)** and the **Low-Income Communities Bonus Credit** — that convert those credits into **direct cash payments from the U.S. Treasury**, even for tax-exempt Tribal entities. The combined stack can reduce a project's net capital cost by **30–50%** or more before state or DOE grant programs are layered on.
